# One-Line Robot Wedding Vows — Candidates

Bounty: `u-kreutzx-one-line-robot-wedding-vows-a154ba4b`
Rules: one line, ≤ 160 characters, funny, robot couple.
Judge criteria (in order): 1) one-line robot wedding vow that lands as a joke;
2) the laugh is in the line itself — a real turn, surprise, or wordplay paying off in the last few words;
3) sounds like a wedding — a promise or commitment, not just a gag;
4) the machine side is specific (sensors, firmware, batteries, updates, backups, ports);
5) fits one line and reads well aloud, rhythm lets the laugh land.

Scoring is strict. A 25/25 must be genuinely champion-level on all five criteria.
No candidate below reached 25/25 — stated plainly. Best score is 24/25.
